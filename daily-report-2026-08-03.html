📔 Diary of a CEO

Steven Bartlett 主持

Secretary Pete Buttigieg: The American Dream Is DEAD. We're Running Out Of Time To Fix It!

3 Aug 2026 · 1h 54m（6880秒）

嘉賓背景

Pete Buttigieg——美國前運輸部長、前 South Bend（印第安納州）市長、Rhodes Scholar、喺阿富汗服役過嘅海軍後備軍官。2020 年參選過總統（當時係首位有真實勝算嘅公開同性戀總統候選人），而家被公認係 2028 年民主黨提名嘅領跑者之一。今次係佢多年嚟首次冇公職在身接受長訪談，講嘢異常坦率。訪談錄於 2026 年 8 月初：當時美國正捲入伊朗戰爭、國會正就戰爭經費對質國防部長 Hegseth、英國正辯論 1,000 萬英鎊以上財富稅。

開場定調：美國夢已死？

Bartlett 開場問「你點睇美國而家嘅狀態」，Buttigieg 冇留餘地：

「I'm really worried about where we're headed as a country. … That whole project is, in my view, it's on the brink.」（我好擔心國家嘅去向。成個計劃——建立一個由人民做主嘅國家——我睇嚟已經瀕臨懸崖邊緣。）

佢將「美國夢」定義為「無論你起步幾低，都可以比起步時過得好」。關鍵數據：

  • 「That used to happen for 9 out of 10 Americans.」（以前十個美國人有九個可以做到）
  • 「Statistically, the place where you're most likely to live out the American dream right now is Denmark. And I want that to be America.」——統計上，而家最大機會實現美國夢嘅地方係丹麥，佢想嗰度係美國
  • 根源：過去 30–40 年嘅政策「tax work more than they tax wealth」（稅制對勞動嘅稅率高過對財富嘅稅率）

佢有一個重要嘅社會學判斷：

「When inequality reaches a certain level and when the inequality of power follows the economic inequality to a certain level, that's when I think you start to have earthquakes in the society… the rise in political violence, the collapse in trust in our society. All of these things I think are related to that, and we're running out of time to do something about it.」（當經濟不平等擴散到權力不平等，社會就會開始地震——政治暴力抬頭、社會信任崩塌，全部都同呢樣嘢有關，而我哋已經冇乜時間剩。）

點解 Trump 仍然有共鳴？（佢對自己選民嘅理解）

  • 「He speaks to a lot of people who know that the system has let them down.」——佢對住一班明知被制度出賣嘅人講嘢
  • 「If the system's let you down, you're only going to vote so many times within that system before somebody comes along and says, 'I'm going to blow it all up.'」——制度令你失望太多次，總會有人話「我要炸毀一切」而你會俾佢一次機會
  • 但佢指出 Trump 嘅承諾係假嘅：支持者信佢「唔會發動新戰爭」、信佢「上任第一日就降物價」——結果「right now inflation's higher than it was the day he took over」（而家通脹高過佢上任當日）
  • 對民主黨嘅教訓：「the answer is not to reverse the damage, put everything back the way it was」——特朗普崛起正正因為舊制度令人失望，民主黨唔可以淨係諗住「還原」
「Our mission can't be to just take power and then go back to what we had before, because what we had before is actually what led to this.」（我哋嘅任務唔可以係攞返權力然後回到從前，因為從前嗰套正正就係搞成今日咁嘅原因。）

2028 會唔會選總統？——被 Bartlett 逼出嚟嘅答案

Bartlett 用自己公司嘅招聘框架逼供（四個選項：strongly not inclined / not inclined / inclined / strongly inclined，用嚟防止人「坐喺欄柵上」）：

「Steven Bartlett: I need to— the inclination.
Pete Buttigieg: I'm obviously not to one end or the other.
Bartlett: Yeah, okay, so you're not those two.
Buttigieg: I think I'm more inclined than not.」（我傾向多過唔傾向。）

但佢強調未係決定：有仔女之後所有計算都變晒——「That's changed all of my equations.」（佢同丈夫 Chasten 領養咗龍鳳胎）。佢揀公職嘅邏輯好個人化：睇「個職位喺嗰個時刻需要咩，而我帶嚟嘅嘢夠唔夠唔同」。當年 20 幾歲選市長就係呢個邏輯。

伊朗戰爭：佢眼中嘅「承諾與背叛」

呢段係全集最政治嘅部分，背景係 2026 年 2 月開始嘅美伊衝突，到 8 月「停火」仍然未落實，霍爾木茲海峽被封鎖，美國有軍人陣亡。佢嘅觀點：

  • 總統將自己迫入牆角：生活成本危機同 Epstein 檔案醜聞令總統壓力極大，戰爭變成「政治上著數」嘅選項——「There was a moment where it seemed to him that he was better off politically if we were at war.」
  • 戰爭目標不停變：最初話「regime change」（政權更迭）、「unconditional surrender」（無條件投降）、又話要處理核計劃——但「last year he said they obliterated the nuclear program」（上年先話已經摧毀咗伊朗核計劃）
  • 最諷刺嘅指控：政府刪走陣亡軍人名單上嘅 4 個名——
「His administration deleted 4 names from the ranks of those killed in action in this conflict because they were afraid that listing their names would contradict him saying that we had a ceasefire.」（佢哋刪走 4 名陣亡軍人嘅名字，因為怕列出嚟會同「我哋有停火」嘅講法矛盾。）
  • 國務卿 Rubio 喺國會話「戰爭結束咗」——「We all know the war's not over.」
  • 戰爭成本：「I think his latest number was $37.5 billion. That's our money, all of us as American taxpayers.」
  • 結果比開戰前更差：油價更高、「the least bad options still involve Iran having more control over the Strait of Hormuz than they were asserting a year ago」
  • 佢用親身經歷回應：「I know what it feels like to get on a gray tail airplane that's taking you into a war zone.」（佢係阿富汗退伍軍人）
「One thing we have learned is that it is harder to stop a war than it is to start one, which is why if you have any other reasonable choice, you don't start one.」（學到嘅教訓：停戰比開戰難得多，所以只要有其他合理選擇，就唔好開戰。）

對伊朗核武立場清晰：「No, they should not be allowed to have nuclear weapons.」 但承認「They may have less capacity, but they certainly have more motivation」——制裁與撕毀協議令伊朗更想擁核。佢批評特朗普撕毀奧巴馬協議但攞唔到更好嘅 deal，摧毀咗美國嘅 credibility。

中東大局：佢對 Netanyahu 評價極狠——「I don't think he is remotely interested in the safety and dignity and security of Palestinian people… he would be better off if they didn't exist」；佢眼中 Netanyahu 嘅真實計劃係 Greater Israel（大以色列）意識形態——「a vision that does not have a lot of room for Palestinians anywhere between the river and the sea」。同時佢重申三件事可以並存：全球猶太人安全、巴勒斯坦人嘅尊嚴（佢形容加沙發生嘅係「war crimes」）、以色列作為猶太國家嘅存續——「I believe in principle they have to be honored at the same time.」

AI：身分危機 vs 公共紅利（全集最有深度嘅一段）

Bartlett 引用各大佬嘅預測：Sam Altman「到 2028 年底，世界上更多智力容量可能喺數據中心入面而唔係外面」；Dario「幾年內 AI 喺所有嘢上都好過人類」；Demis「十年內通用人類級智能」；Musk「工作會變成一個選擇」；Uber CEO Dara「900 萬司機將被取代」。Buttigieg 嘅回應框架：

「Either we're looking at a future where because of AI, we have a shorter work week and more money in our pocket, or we're looking at a future where there is even more extreme concentration of wealth and power in this country than we've already got. And which way it goes… it's not a tech question. It's a policy choice.」（AI 嘅未來只有兩條路：更短工時加更多錢入口袋，或者財富權力更加極端集中。行邊條路唔係技術問題，係政策選擇。）

佢最有力嘅論點係「身分」（identity）而唔係「工資」：

  • 以佢家鄉 Indiana 汽車工業為例：90 年代工人失業後「never recovered their sense of identity」——「It's how your spouses know each other from the union picnic. It's how you're sponsoring Little League.」
  • 「At least in the US, we don't even say, what's your job? We say, what do you do? … Basically as a way of saying, who are you?」——美國人問「你做咩」其實係問「你係邊個」
  • 「There is a generation of workers who are about to have their identity, not just their job, their identity disrupted.」
  • 引用 Keynes 1930 年《Economic Possibilities for Our Grandchildren》：預言生產力升 5–8 倍、2030 年人類只需每週工作 10–15 小時——「So far we are on track to almost perfectly match his prediction about productivity, but we're nowhere close to that. If anything, we're working more right now.」——生產力預言啱晒，工時預言完全錯，中間嘅落差就係政策選擇
  • 連 Seneca（羅馬斯多葛哲學家）都出場：人對金錢極度計較，但對時間——「the one thing where you have every reason to be even more stingy」——反而任人浪費，而家啲時間被「bullshit scrolling」吞噬

解決方案——「公共紅利」：

「Imagine some combination of artificial intelligence and robotics that leads to factories where you can kind of do that [Star Trek replicator]. … More and more is going to depend on, well, who actually owns the machine, by which I mean both the intellectual property, the AI, and the physical plant. And that's where we've got to make sure that the American people own enough of this.」（當工廠唔再需要人，經濟就取決於「邊個擁有部機器」——包括 AI 知識產權同實體廠房。必須確保美國人民擁有足夠份額。）
  • 主張某種「dividend」（全民紅利）：「there's some public share. We can argue about whether it should come with a vote… or whether it's kind of closer to a tax」
  • 正當性論證：AI 用嘅數據「all of us put in the world for free」；互聯網本身係美國納稅人 60 年代經 DARPA 出錢研發——「now it's time to return more of that to the American people」
  • 對 Bernie Sanders「全民持有 50% AI 公司股份 + 董事會投票權」方案：部分認同（公共利潤份額合理），但董事會 vs 監管規則要斟酌——佢用 FAA 類比：FAA 唔造飛機，但訂立安全規則，結果佢任內「4 billion passenger enplanements… the number of fatalities was zero. 0 out of 4 billion.」——「There are very few things you can do in life, all the way down to crossing the street or eating a cheeseburger, that would have a 0 to 4 billion fatality rate.」

財富稅：億萬富豪交稅率低過老師同消防員

  • 「if you are a multi-billionaire, you probably pay a lower percentage of what you make in taxes than if you're a schoolteacher or a firefighter」
  • 「We even in percentage terms generally tax billionaires less than we tax millionaires」——越有錢稅率越低
  • 歷史先例：40–60 年代美國最富裕稅階邊際稅率高達 90%——「I'm not saying we need to go back to that. I'm just saying that was happening… when the US represented capitalism against communism」——即係話「資本主義 + 高稅率」有先例，目的唔係懲罰富人，而係防止財富轉化成足以拖冧成個制度嘅政治權力
  • 財富稅定義：「a property tax by another name」——合法，但執行難（點樣稅一幅藝術收藏？）
  • Bartlett 親身爆料：27 歲時有人教佢用上市公司股票做抵押借稅務貸款——「They will just transfer you today $25 million in cash and you'll have to pay no tax… This is what Elon's doing.」——Buttigieg 回應：
「There's always going to be a cat and mouse… But if the idea that somebody will figure out a way around this law meant that you shouldn't have the law, that would be an argument against having any law.」（永遠係貓捉老鼠。但如果因為有人會繞過法律就唔應該有法律，咁所有法律都唔使要。）

仲有一句全場最金：

「Right now the easiest way to get a billion dollars is to have $10 billion and do nothing.」（而家賺 10 億美金最易嘅方法：擁有 100 億美金然後乜都唔做。）

回應 AOC 嘅「You can't make a billion dollars, you take a billion dollars」——「That doesn't make a ton of sense to me… the thing you did between zero and a billion is usually much more impressive than whatever you did between $1 billion and $10 billion」；回應「system that allows billionaires to exist is immoral」——「It's not whether they exist, it's having too much power… turning economic power into political power」。

國債與 Stein's Law

Bartlett 展示 1970–2026 國債圖（「looks like a rocket is taking off」），並引用 Ray Dalio 嘅 80 年大國週期論。Buttigieg 提出 Stein's Law（ Reagan 時代經濟學家 Herbert Stein，即《Ferris Bueller》Ben Stein 個老竇）：

「If something cannot go on forever, it will stop. … I believe one of two things can happen, reform or catastrophe. And the whole reason I'm involved in public life is to try to make sure that it's reform and not catastrophe.」（如果一件事冇可能永遠持續，佢就會停。之後只會有兩個結局：改革或者災難。）
  • 稅收分析：由 2001 年 Bush 減稅開始計，加埋 Trump 嘅富人減稅——「if it weren't for all those tax cuts, the debt would actually be going down right now」
  • 國債嘅危險：「It could be a cause of a currency crisis or force us to redo our whole tax code within the next few years」
  • 回應 Bartlett 嘅「政治音樂椅」論（政客 4 年任期 vs 20 年災難）：「Are you in office just in order to hold it? … Unless you understand that you hold an office in order to do things with policy and power to make people better off, not the other way around.」

制度改革清單：選舉人團、最高法院、Citizens United

佢最 radical 嘅主張全部係「制度維修」而唔係經濟政策：

  • 廢除選舉人團、改為普選：「There's nothing radical about thinking the person who gets the most votes ought to be the winner.」——好處：民主黨要去德州拉票、共和黨要去紐約，細州都有人理
  • 禁 gerrymandering（選區劃分操縱）：「We have 435 seats in the House. 9 out of 10 are so red or so blue that you basically know who's going to win before the first vote is cast.」——呢個係 50-50 嘅國家，但 9 成選區係安全區，政客只驚自己黨內初選
  • 「There's like 60 or 70% agreement on most of the issues… and we fail to get those policies done」——全民 6、7 成共識嘅議題（全民醫保、最低工資、富人稅、槍械背景審查）全部做唔到，證明制度壞咗
  • 最高法院改革：法官任期限制、增加法官數目、「Balanced Bench Proposal」，令提名冇咁黨派化
  • 眾議院比例代表制：大選區、每個選民投多個候選人、top 3 當選，令 gerrymandering 難啲
  • 修憲推翻 Citizens United：「corporate spending is not speech and corporations are not citizens」——恢復 McCain-Feingold 時代嘅競選獻金限制；佢提醒：「the best thing about the Constitution is its capability to be amended」——第一修正案本身就係 amendment，女性同黑人投票權都係靠修憲
「So how can we be a country that's got 60 or 70% agreement on issue after issue after issue and we fail to get those policies done and we keep proceeding like it's normal to be a 50-50 country where everybody's at each other's throats? That to me is evidence of a broken system.」（一個對議題有 6、7 成共識嘅國家，點解乜都做唔到，仲要當「五五開、互相廝殺」係正常？呢個就係制度壞咗嘅證據。）

Elon Musk 與 DOGE

  • 總評價：「Very intelligent, has had an extremely important impact… is right about a lot of things and is very wrong about a lot of things and lacked the humility to understand some things that would've saved him from doing a lot of damage」
  • DOGE 失敗最令佢嬲嘅係：政府效率改革本身係需要嘅，但「what we got was unbelievably sloppy, making math errors」——「saying billion when they meant million on the wall of receipts」；炒人唔知人哋做咩（包括核武安全、生物危害管理崗位）之後又要急急請返；「leaving a federal government that actually spent more after Doge than before」
  • 2021 年白宮電動車峰會冇請 Tesla：佢承認「That was a mistake. I was there for that.」——原因係當時峰會係為咗慶祝 Big 3 嘅 tailpipe 協議，而 Tesla 冇 tailpipe，所以根本冇人諗起——「What Elon saw was there's an event at the White House about EVs. He is… the person most responsible for EV excellence on American soil. And he's saying, 'What the f—?' And I don't blame him for that.」
  • 合作哲學：佢喺運輸部同 Elon 有具體衝突時直接打電話——「we talked it out and we figured it out and we actually solved the problem together. It is amazing in my experience how many problems can be… solved if you just pick up the phone.」

2028 黨內對手：Kamala、Newsom、AOC、Biden 換人

  • 四大領跑者（佢、Newsom、AOC、Kamala），Bartlett 已訪問 3 個，淨低 AOC
  • 佢拒絕評論「如果唔係你會揀邊個」——「It's just a bad idea to answer that question」
  • Kamala：佢拍檔最多（基建法案），「I campaigned for her very energetically… I thought she'd be a very good president」
  • Newsom：「understands how to break through in this kind of attention world」
  • AOC：「activated a lot of people who were looking around saying, 'The way things work doesn't serve me'」——但基建法案上佢哋有分歧
  • Biden 認知衰退：佢承認「I saw the same thing that America saw… he was getting older」，辯論係「the beginning, of course, of the process that led him to reach the same conclusion」；但佢冇被邀請入「佢會唔會再選」嘅決策房
  • 換 Kamala 決定（事後孔明）：「I think it's pretty clear that we would've been better off if we had that [a competition]」——如果當時有初選式競爭，即使最後都係 Kamala，「she likely would've been a better candidate」，因為「competition sharpens you」

個人故事：父親、出櫃、領養——點解呢啲塑造佢嘅世界觀

  • 老竇由馬爾他移民美國，導師話佢留低會係「大池細魚」，佢答：「I'd rather be a medium-sized fish in a very big pond」（寧願做大池嘅中魚）
  • 喺 South Bend 長大，鄰居有鋪地毯工人、退休教師、護士；城市因為 Studebaker（當年同 GM/Ford 齊名嘅車廠）1963 年倒閉而一蹶不振——「factories that hadn't been operating for decades… looked like they'd been hit in a war」——細個接收到嘅世界訊息係：「If you want to be someone, you have to get out.」
  • 老竇喺佢宣布競選總統嗰週去世（確診癌症 3–4 個月後），同一時期佢媽媽心臟病發做手術——佢仍然喺度做緊市長
  • 出櫃故事：後備軍官時期「if I was known to be gay, I'd be fired」；去阿富汗部署前要寫「萬一返唔嚟」嘅信——「I'm a grown man in a position of responsibility… I don't know what it's like to be in love. And I just thought, that's untenable.」佢應承自己平安返嚟就要面對——返嚟之後揀咗主動公開（「I definitely didn't want to be 'found out'」）
  • 同 Chasten 領養龍鳳胎：「The hardest I've ever worked in my life was when I was on parental leave」——凌晨 3 點起身，同丈夫輪更；佢主張 paid family leave 同照顧者稅務寬免，因為「that's work too」
  • 國家服務（national service）主張：唔一定要參軍，但「everybody should know what it's like to be part of something bigger than yourself」
