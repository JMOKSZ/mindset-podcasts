2026-08-23 · 靈感來源：Claude Hopkins 自傳《My Life In Advertising》+ 經典《Scientific Advertising》

🎯 主題

David Senra 用一整集拆解史上最偉大廣告文案寫手 Claude Hopkins 嘅一生同方法論。Hopkins 嘅價值高到一個地步：當佢將畢生所學寫成書，佢廣告公司嘅創辦人竟然將手稿鎖喺保險箱 20年 唔俾出版——因為佢哋驚競爭對手學晒啲精髓。書終於出版後賣咗 超過800萬本，David Ogilvy（廣告界另一個傳奇）話：「任何未將本書讀足七次嘅人，都唔應該做廣告。」

💡 核心論點（附原文引用）

1. 非凡工時 = 非凡成就

"The man who works twice as long as his fellows is bound to go twice as far, especially in advertising."
"If I have gone higher than others in advertising or done more, the fact is not due to exceptional ability but to exceptional hours."

2. 愛上工作——工作同玩樂嘅界線只係心態

"Which is work and which is play? If a thing is useful, they call it work. If it's useless, they call it play. But one is hard as the other. All the difference I see lies in the attitude of the mind."
"I came to love work as other men love golf. I love it still. The love of work can be cultivated just like the love of play."

3. 好產品自己會賣——樣本比說話有力

"A good product is its own best salesman. It is uphill work to sell goods without samples."

4. 永遠唔好用自己標準判斷市場

"We must never judge humanity by ourselves. The things we want, the things we like may appeal to a small minority."

5. 廣告要接受公眾審判——唯測試係真理

"We must submit all things in advertising to the court of public opinion."

6. 唔好賣嘢，要賣服務——自私嘅推銷只會惹反感

"The average salesman seeks favors. He seeks profit for himself. His plea is, 'Buy my goods.' He makes a selfish appeal to selfish people, and of course, he meets resistance. I was selling service."

7. Schlitz 啤酒經典案例——「我賦予咗純淨一個意義」

"Others have never told this story. It will startle everyone in print. I gave purity a meaning." (On Schlitz Beer jumping from 5th to 1st.)

Hopkins 參觀釀酒廠後發現：廠房嘅玻璃房、純淨水、消毒工序——其他競爭者都有，但從來冇人喺廣告講。佢將「製造過程嘅用心」講出嚟，令 Schlitz 由第5名躍升第1名。

8. 講出你為卓越付出嘅努力——製造商離產品太近反而睇唔到

"Tell the pains that you take to excel. The maker is too close to his product. He sees in his methods only the ordinary."

9. 廣告嘅兩大死罪：吹噓同自私

"The greatest two faults in advertising lie in boasts and in selfishness. People will listen if you talk service to them. They will turn their backs when you seek to impress an advantage for yourself."

10. 人只會為「解除痛苦」行動，唔會為「預防」行動

"People will do anything to cure a trouble, but little to prevent it. Prevention offers slight appeal to humanity in general. They want to be told of rewards."

11. 天才不過係願意落苦功

"Genius is the art of taking pains. The advertising man who spares the midnight oil will never get very far. This is no lazy man's field."
